Last week, our paper "PETALE: a Fine-Grained Evaluation Framework for Process Modeling Methods" was presented by Clara Kowalczyk at the ER2026 conference happening in St John's. Co-authored with Alexis Brissard, Pr. Cuppens and Pr. Zouaq, this paper offers a new evaluation framework for business process models.

The conference was a great opportunity to share our work with the process modeling community. We enjoyed discussing new perspectives and exploring potential directions for future research.

Many thanks to all our co-authors for their collaboration, and to everyone who contributed to the discussions!

Find the paper [HERE](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-40795-5_33).
